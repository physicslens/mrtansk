How To Commander

Welcome to Tactical Commander! You are in command of an elite brigade tasked with defending the western lines from constant aggressive waves of enemy forces approaching from the east.

🛡️ The Core Symmetrical Economy:

  • Your treasury generates resources automatically every second.
  • Fair Play: The enemy generates resources at the exact same rate (+15 gold/sec) and is constrained by the exact same recruitment costs!
  • Enemies spend their gold to deploy counter-brigades matching your troop tiers and advance across the map.

⚔️ Formations and Ordering Layouts:

  • Line Formation (1): Layers vertical ranks abreast facing the gates in the order: Cavalry ➔ Swordsmen ➔ Spearmen ➔ Archers.
  • V-Shape Formation (2): Creates a tactical charging wedge where the strongest apex leads forward.
  • Box Formation (3): Forms a defensive hollow wall structure keeping all vulnerable Archers safe inside the center.

🎮 Tactical Combat Controls:

  • Box-Selecting: Hold left-click on the map and drag your mouse to create a selection box.
  • Shift+Click: Hold Shift and tap individual soldiers to select/deselect them.
  • Right-Clicking: Select units, then right-click on the map to issue custom movement destinations.
